# Threat Model Document

## 1. Threat Factors

### Human Detection
Weight: 35

Description:
Detection of unauthorized human movement in a monitored sector.

### Vehicle Detection
Weight: 25

Description:
Detection of suspicious vehicle activity near the border.

### Visibility
Weight: 10

Description:
Low visibility due to fog, darkness, smoke, or dust.

### Historical Risk
Weight: 15

Description:
Sector has a history of intrusion attempts or suspicious activity.

### Patrol Gap
Weight: 10

Description:
Long duration since the last patrol in the sector.

### Weather
Weight: 5

Description:
Adverse weather conditions that may increase infiltration risk.

---

## 2. Threat Scoring Logic

| Factor | Score |
|----------|---------|
| Human Detection | +35 |
| Vehicle Detection | +25 |
| Low Visibility | +10 |
| Historical Risk | +15 |
| Patrol Gap > 4 Hours | +10 |
| Severe Weather | +5 |

### Example Calculation

Human Detection + Historical Risk + Low Visibility

Score:

35 + 15 + 10 = 60

Threat Level = Medium

## 4. Threat Response Levels

| Score Range | Threat Level |
|-------------|--------------|
| 0 - 30 | Safe |
| 31 - 60 | Medium |
| 61 - 80 | High |
| 81 - 100 | Critical |

### Response Actions

Safe:
- Continue monitoring

Medium:
- Notify operator

High:
- Increase surveillance
- Send patrol alert

Critical:
- Immediate command center notification
- Emergency response activation

---

## 5. Explainability Format

```json
{
  "score": 85,
  "reasons": [
    "Human detected",
    "Historical high-risk sector",
    "Low visibility"
  ]
}
```

Purpose:

Every threat score generated by the system must include a list of factors contributing to the score to improve transparency and operator trust.
